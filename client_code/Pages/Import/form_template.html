Take everything with you

No lock-in. Any member can download the whole crew at any time: cards with their history and comments, epics, sprints and retros, requests, settings. Import the JSON into another AutoAgile crew, or open the CSV in a spreadsheet or another tracker.

The CSV uses Jira-style column names, so other trackers read it too.

What comes along
Kept
Titles, descriptions, types, priorities, points, labels, epics, comments, links back, and the dates that make your history count.
People
Matched to crew members by email or name. Anyone else stays in the card text.
Twice?
Safe. Cards already imported (same key) are skipped.
Points
Snapped to the nearest rung (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21). Run the gates later to make them comparable.
