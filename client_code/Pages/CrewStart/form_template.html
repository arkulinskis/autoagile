Where do you start?

Quarter and year planning unlock from your own data, whatever you pick.

The four levels

1 · KANBAN. Young crews start here: a ranked backlog, a board, WIP limits. You learn your rhythm before you promise anything.

2 · SCRUM. When 25 cards and 6 steady weeks are done: sprints with goals, planned against real velocity, and retros.

3 · QUARTER. After 3 steady sprints: a 3-month forecast of epics with a confidence range.

4 · YEAR. After 6 predictable sprints: a year roadmap in quarters, estimated from measured capacity.

Complexity gates estimate every card the same way, so points mean something by the time you plan a year.
